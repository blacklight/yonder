Timelines & posts

Everything a microblog should do — and more

Home, local and federated timelines update in real time over WebSocket streaming, while posts get the full Mastodon toolkit plus a few extras.

  • Rich posts — plain text or Markdown, content warnings, media with editable alt text, and multi-line image descriptions
  • Polls, quotes and emoji reactions — quote posts with author authorization, and reactions that federate to Akkoma and Misskey
  • Scheduled posts, edits and drafts — write now, publish later, fix typos after the fact, keep drafts across devices
  • Followed hashtags, trends and lists — plus server-side filters (warn/hide/blur) that apply everywhere
Threads & conversations

Discussions that don't fall apart

Opening a post fetches its whole reply tree from the origin server, and remote like/boost counts come along when the origin publishes them — no half-empty conversations.

  • Remote thread expansion — replies discovered on demand. You always see the full picture, not only what your instance has cached
  • Direct messages as conversations — grouped threads, unread badges. Direct messages are conversations with people, not just a scrollable timeline of private posts
  • Remote interaction lists — see who liked or boosted a remote post, proxied from its home server. Get the full picture even if your server doesn't have many peers
  • Webmentions — public posts send and receive Webmentions, so blogs are part of the conversation too. Mention a blog article, and the author will be notified. And mentions of your social posts on Webmentions-compatible websites get rendered as normal comments in your conversation
Federation & clients

Your apps already speak Yonder

The Mastodon client API — v1 and v2 — is implemented end to end, so the ecosystem around Mastodon works against a Yonder instance out of the box.

  • Mastodon-compatible API — Tusky, Ivory, Elk, Phanpy and friends just need your instance URL
  • Full ActivityPub via pubby-sdk — interoperates with Mastodon, Akkoma, Pleroma, Misskey and other Yonder instances
  • Real-time streaming — Mastodon's WebSocket protocol for user, public, hashtag and list streams
  • Feeds & embeds — RSS 2.0 and Atom for profiles, tags and lists, plus embeddable status cards and oEmbed
Migration & portability

Your identity isn't a hostage

Moving accounts is a first-class feature, not an emergency procedure — in both directions, with your history intact.

  • Moves in either direction — signed ActivityPub Move handling, follower migration with retries and progress, and moved-account banners — compatible with Mastodon and Pleroma/Akkoma
  • Mastodon-compatible archives — export posts, media, follows, bookmarks and lists, with signed downloads — even for suspended accounts, which get export-only access
  • Import from anywhere — upload an archive or connect a remote account over OAuth and import posts, favourites, bookmarks and follows, preserving timestamps and provenance
  • Move your data in a couple of clicks — move your whole social story, not only your followers. Yonder allows you to connect over OAuth to your old instance and decide what you want to import. No need to download and upload CSV files. No more posts and interactions left behind upon migration
  • Remote historical context — imported posts keep their original date and like/boost counts where available
Your instance, your rules

A community you actually run

Every registration policy, every moderation action, every setting — through the admin panel or straight from the yonder CLI.

  • Moderation toolbox — user mutes/blocks/reports (forwarded via Flag), actor limits and suspension, domain blocks, relay management
  • Registration your way — open, invite-only, approval-required or closed, with managed invites
  • Custom emoji & packs — upload, import and organize instance emoji that render consistently everywhere
  • Full customizable experience — multi-language by design, accent and theme customization, and support for custom frontends (Pleroma/Akkoma compatible)
And there's more

Built like a scalable social platform, not a pet project

A single Rust binary, PostgreSQL and Redis — the unglamorous pieces are already where they should be.

🦀

One binary

No Ruby, no Elixir, no sidekiq fleet — a single Rust process plus PostgreSQL and Redis runs the whole instance.

🔑

OAuth2 provider

Authorization-code + PKCE, device flow, password and client-credentials grants, plus scoped revocable API tokens.

🖥️

Pluggable frontends

The bundled Vue SPA is the default — alternative frontends install from the CLI and serve side by side. The bundle PWA provides a curated mobile experience even without the need for an native mobile app.

📖

Open API

OpenAPI 3 at /openapi.json and a bundled Swagger UI at /swagger-ui — Mastodon-shaped contracts throughout.

🔒

Safety by default

Argon2id passwords, SSRF-guarded remote fetches, Redis-backed rate limits and durable at-least-once federation delivery.

🔔

Notifications your way

Real-time in-app, Web Push and email — with per-type preferences and daily digests where you want them.

🛜

Feeds & Webmentions

RSS 2.0 and Atom for profiles, tags and lists — and bidirectional Webmentions for public posts.

🧭

Remote discovery

Look up remote actors, posts and interactions by handle or URL — SSRF-guarded, moderated, cached.
